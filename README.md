# Cybersecurity Portfolio

## About Me

I served twelve and a half years in the U.S. Army as a combat medic and have spent the past twelve years providing foster care for medically fragile adults. Cybersecurity has always interested me, but my lack of technical experience prevented me from ever pursuing it.

I am currently pursuing a B.S. in Applied Cybersecurity at SANS Technology Institute to develop the knowledge and practical skills necessary to continue helping and protecting others in a new way.

## NCL Fall 2026 Experience and Reflection

Participating in the Fall 2026 National Cyber League (NCL) Individual and Team Games gave me an opportunity to apply what I've learned at SANS and identify areas where my training needs improvement. I particularly enjoyed the OSINT, cryptography, and password cracking sections. The other categories challenged me to research unfamiliar concepts, learn new tools and methodologies, and improve my approach to problem-solving and note-taking.

The Team Game reinforced the importance of communication, documenting work and progress, and supporting findings with evidence so teammates can independently verify the results. My biggest takeaway was that finding the answer or flag was only half the solution. Being able to explain how that answer was obtained is just as important.

One principle I've carried with me from my military experience is:

> "We don't rise to meet expectations. We default to the level of our training."

NCL reinforced the importance of hands-on practice and continuing education to maintain proficiency. Cybersecurity is a perishable skill, and I still have a lot to learn.
